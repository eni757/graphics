## Earth image

The earth image (earth.png) is originally from the JPL (Jet Propulsion
Laboratory) "Looking at Earth: From 100 miles to 100 million miles" gallery:
  https://explorer1.jpl.nasa.gov/galleries/earth-from-space/
The images are released under the "JPL Image Use Policy", which permits use of
the images for any purpose without prior permission (subject to special cases
listed on the website). See 
  https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy
for further information.

The image has been edited slightly, by adding an alpha mask around the earth
and has then been scaled to a lower resolution, appropriate for the use in the
coursework.

## Goddard and Phalanx

The Goddard (goddard8x8.png) and Phalanx (phalanx.png) images are created from
Naev's assets:
  https://codeberg.org/naev/naev-assets-lossy/
Naev attributes them to Vega Strike
  https://www.vega-strike.org/
The assets are released under a GPLv2+ license. The original GLTF input 3d
models are available in the Naev Codeberg repository.
